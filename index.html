Synthesis

Key findings

01
The core problem is validated at scale — 78% experience unplanned Instagram use weekly or more, with 59% saying "almost every session."
02
Planning Fallacy is the most strongly evidenced trigger in the dataset — 81% either don't estimate or underestimate session length, and 70% let a "quick" break run long. The Over-Committer branch has the best data support of the four.
03
The dominant recovery mechanism people already use is awareness, not tools — guilt beats timers and social nudges by more than 2×. This validates interruption-based design more than any single reward mechanic.
Honest limitation

Only 11% cited FOMO/disconnection as their primary trigger (Q2), versus 37% for pacing/boredom and 44% for general fatigue. The Connection-Seeker persona — built as the project's primary persona — represents a smaller share of self-reported causes than initially weighted. With more time, a next iteration would likely re-balance emphasis toward the Pacing and general-fatigue drivers, though the app's branch structure already covers all four and needs no rebuild to act on this.
